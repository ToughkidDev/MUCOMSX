| 2026-11-25 | WEB | **[MUCPlayground](https://toughkiddev.github.io/MUCOMSX/MUCPlayground/)**: a note tied on after a portamento in a converted **MGS file** sounds where MGSDRV leaves it. MGSDRV sets no pitch for a tied note that is the note it played last, and a portamento does not count, so `f _f f+ & f` stays on f+; the converter wrote the f, as a slide tied on to its own first note (`{ff+}32 &f16.`), which MucoMSX's compiler and this page's engine each join to a different note before it, so MUCPLAY and the page played it differently (`DSA_10` started two notes a loop 0.12 s late on the MSX). Ten of the 844 test songs change; in the mucoMSX emulator all ten play their notes where the engine does (`DSA_10` within 6 ms). MDX conversions write a slide tied on to its first note the same way. |
